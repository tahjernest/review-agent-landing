Questions

The things everybody asks.

Isn't filtering out bad reviews against Google's rules?

Review Agent doesn't filter or block anyone. Every client gets the same question, and nobody is stopped from leaving a review. The difference is that an unhappy client gets a real response from you first — which is just good service. Many of them end up leaving a positive review after you fix the problem.

Do I have to do anything after an appointment?

No. Review Agent connects to your booking system and fires on its own when an appointment is marked complete. If you don't use booking software, you can send it off a simple client list instead.

What if a client replies with a real question instead of a number?

It handles the normal back-and-forth on its own and hands the conversation to you the moment it's something a human should answer — a refund, a complaint, a rebooking request.

Will it text my clients too much?

One message per visit, with a built-in cooldown so regulars who come in every two weeks aren't asked every single time. Anyone can opt out by replying STOP.

How long does setup take?

Most shops are live the same day. We need your Google review link, your booking system login, and about fifteen minutes of your time.
